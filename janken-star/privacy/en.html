Overview

Janken Star (the "App") is an online game in which players across Japan play one-on-one rock-paper-scissors. The operator (ryozowski, an individual) collects information about users only within the scope of this Policy and uses it only for the purposes stated here.

  • Outcomes, records, ranks, and icon designs are decided only by the operator's servers (Google Firebase). The App therefore sends match and settings information to the servers
  • Exchanges between users (the hands played and the phrases sent) are treated as "secrecy of communications" under the Telecommunications Business Act (see "Protecting the Secrecy of Communications")
  • The App includes advertising (Google AdMob) and usage measurement and crash reporting (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics). What is sent and to whom is listed under "External Transmission (Disclosure under the Telecommunications Business Act)"
  • This Policy covers the iOS version. The Android version is in preparation, and this Policy will be revised before it is offered

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match players, run and judge duels, exchange phrases, and provide records, magnitudes, rankings, awards, icon designs, and Star Letters
  • Check for duplicate nicknames, prevent impersonation, handle reports, and support blocking
  • Prevent fraudulent use and unfair awards, including the one-account-per-device check and the monthly review of top players
  • Verify Starry Sky Premium purchases and provide its benefits
  • Deliver notifications (only if you allow them)
  • Respond to outages and inquiries, and announce service suspensions and update requests
  • Improve the service so it runs smoothly, such as adjusting the grace period added to the 10-second deadline for choosing a hand
  • Understand usage and improve quality (measurement not linked to your account)
  • Show ads (if you are not subscribed to Starry Sky Premium)

Protecting the Secrecy of Communications

Information that forms communications between users — the hands played and phrases sent, who played whom and when, reports and blocks, and joining the matchmaking queue (the "secrecy of communications") — is handled as follows under the Telecommunications Business Act.

  • We record it only as far as needed to provide the service: providing duels and phrases, records and rankings, reviewing top players and preventing fraud, handling reports, responding to outages and inquiries, and adjusting the time allowed for choosing a hand
  • Judging duels, copying match rooms, building rankings, computing review flags, and sending notifications are done automatically by server programs, and no person looks at them
  • Only the operator looks at this information, and only for the following purposes and only as far as each purpose requires
    • The monthly review of top players (for award candidates flagged automatically, such as a bias in their opponents)
    • Handling reports (the reported nickname and the number of reports)
    • Investigating fraudulent use (such as abnormal numbers of operations) and responding to outages (the state of a match)
    • Responding to an inquiry from you (the state of that match, with the opponent's information only as far as needed)
    • Deleting accounts and restoring data (a list of account IDs, without looking at the contents)
    • Responding to requests based on law (such as warrants)
  • We never look at it out of curiosity, to analyze a particular person's hand habits, or for promotion. We do not look at the phrases or hands except as needed for the purposes above
  • We do not give it to third parties, except as required by law or when urgently needed to protect a person's life or body. Firebase (Google) is used as the facility to which we entrust storage and processing
  • Retention periods are listed under "Retention Periods", and data past its period is deleted in turn
  • When the operator looks at this information, a record of the purpose and the kinds of information viewed is kept

The overall share of hands and your opponents' hand habits are never shown to anyone. Your most-played hand is shown only in your own Star Letter.

Device Permissions

The App asks for OS permission only for the following three. If you decline any of them, everything except that feature still works.

  • Notifications: To tell you when Today's 3 Duels are back, the day before the month closes, about the announcement and Star Letters, and about astronomical event nights. We ask when you move to Home after the practice duel that follows first-time setup, and when you open the notification settings if we have not asked yet
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): Permission to use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for ads. We ask when a player not subscribed to Starry Sky Premium returns to Home from a Star Duel result. Ads are still shown if you decline, but the IDFA is not sent
  • Adding to Photos: When you tap "Save Image" in Share. We ask only to add images, never to read your photos

Security Measures

To prevent leakage, loss, or damage of the information we collect, the operator takes the following measures.

  • Encrypting communications (HTTPS) and limiting use from anything other than the genuine App with App Check
  • Using database rules so that users can read and write only their own information, and so that match records, reports, and review records are handled only by the servers
  • Limiting access to production data to the operator, and locking the screens and encrypting the disks of the operator's devices
  • Not writing nicknames, hands, purchase IDs, or notification text to server logs
  • Not placing personal information in services outside the operator's devices (such as chat or shared folders)

If a leak or similar incident occurs, we will report it to the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications and the Personal Information Protection Commission and notify the affected users, as required by law.

Deleting Your Data

  • You can delete your account at any time from "Delete Account" in "My Star" in the App. If you cannot use the App, see the Account Deletion Request page, which also lists what is deleted and what remains
  • When you delete from the App, before deletion we revoke the Apple connection for accounts connected to Apple, and release that iPhone's one-account-per-device binding
  • Deleting the App alone does not delete your account
  • Settings on your device are removed when you delete the App

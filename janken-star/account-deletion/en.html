Account Deletion Request

Last updated: November 14, 2026

  • Request Deletion Without Using the App
  • Delete from the App
  • Data That Is Deleted
  • Data That Is Retained

You can delete your Janken Star account (operator: ryozowski) and its related data in the following ways.

Request Deletion Without Using the App

If you cannot use the App, please try the following first. Either one lets you delete your account from within the App.

  • On the same iPhone: Reinstall the App and it opens the same account (the sign-in state stays in the iPhone Keychain)
  • If you connected an Apple or Google account: Install the App on another iPhone and sign in with the same Apple or Google account from "Use an existing account" in first-time setup

If neither works, email us at the address below with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

pontapon.support@gmail.com

  • In your email, please include your nickname and the approximate date you last played
  • To prevent deletion by third parties, we delete an account only after confirming that you are its holder. If you connected a Google account, please send the email from that address. If it matches the connected address, we send a confirmation email to that address. We treat you as the account holder once you reply with the confirmation code in that email and the words "I agree"
  • Because App accounts have no registered contact information such as an email address, we cannot confirm the holder of an account not connected to Google by email, and may be unable to delete it. Anonymous accounts (not connected to Apple or Google) that have not been used for about 13 months since last use may be deleted by the operator
  • We delete the account within 30 days of confirming that you are its holder, and let you know by email

Delete from the App

  1. Open "My Star" in the navigation at the bottom of the App
  2. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm the deletion

For accounts connected to Apple, an Apple sign-in screen appears before deletion (to revoke the Apple connection). Deletion usually finishes right away. If it stops partway, the rest is deleted the next time you open the App, or at the latest one day later.

Data That Is Deleted

  • Your account (sign-in information and the link to the connected Apple or Google account, including your Google email address, display name, and profile photo URL)
  • Your nickname, appearance and settings, records, icon designs, awards, Star Letters, blocked players, and the list of players you reported
  • Your own daily records (such as Today's 3 Duels), records before and after a result, and your matchmaking state
  • The result of the one-account-per-device check and top-player review records
  • Device registrations for notifications and Starry Sky Premium purchase records
  • Your nickname in the ranking lists and in the Star Chart (past First Stars) is cleared

When you delete your account from the App, we also remove that iPhone's mark that "an account eligible for the rankings was bound" (except for accounts permanently excluded in the top-player review and accounts deleted by an email request). If you delete your account during a duel, that duel is closed as a loss. Measurement data on your device is also cleared and the measurement ID is recreated.

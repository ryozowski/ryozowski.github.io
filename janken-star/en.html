FAQ

Detailed questions about how to play, magnitudes, rankings, and icons are answered in the App under Settings (the gear at the top right) → FAQ. Here are answers that help even when you cannot open the App.

How many times can I play a day?

You can play up to 3 Star Duels a day, and they come back at 5:00 every morning (Japan time). Unused duels do not carry over. A duel is used when an opponent is found; it is not used if matching times out (60 seconds) or you cancel. You can play the Practice Duel against the CPU as often as you like from "How to play" in Settings.

I can't find an opponent

Star Duels are played in real time with people playing at the same time. If no one is found within 60 seconds, matching times out and no duel is used. You are matched with the same opponent only once a day, so please try again later.

Can I carry my records over to a new phone?

If you connect an Apple or Google account in "My Star" in the App, you can carry your account over from "Use an existing account" in first-time setup on your new iPhone. You can move to a new device at most once every 30 days. Accounts that are not connected cannot be carried over, so please connect one before changing phones.

My wins don't appear in the rankings

Only one account per device appears in the rankings. Second and later accounts on the same device can still play Star Duels, but their wins do not appear in the rankings. If your account is not eligible, the reason is shown on Home and in the rankings. When an ineligible account becomes eligible, its wins appear from the following month.

How do I cancel Starry Sky Premium?

Cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the App or your account does not cancel it. If you change phones or create a new account, you can move your subscription with Settings → "Restore purchases" in the App. Starry Sky Premium only removes ads and adds appearance options; it never affects the number of duels, ranks, or your chances of winning.

I found an inappropriate nickname

Tap "…" on your opponent's row on the result screen and choose "Report Name" or "Block". A reported name is immediately shown as "Nameless Star" on your screen, and the operator reviews it. If you notice it later, please contact us at the address below with the nickname and the approximate date and time.

I don't get notifications

Check each notification and its delivery time in Settings → "Notification settings" in the App. Please also check that notifications for Janken Star are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

I want to delete my account

You can delete it from "Delete Account" in "My Star" in the App. Deleting the App alone does not delete your account. If you cannot use the App, or to see what is deleted and what remains, see the Account Deletion Request page.

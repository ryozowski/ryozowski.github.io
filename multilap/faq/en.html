Getting Started

How do I add a card?

Tap the “+” below the last card to open the name dialog, then tap OK to add one card. Leave “Keep adding” on and the dialog reopens with an empty field so you can keep going. Each screen holds up to 50 cards.

See it in the guide →

What is the difference between Start, Stop, Lap and Reset?

Start begins timing. Stop pauses it, and pressing Start again resumes from where it stopped. Lap closes the current interval on the spot and immediately starts the next one. Reset clears every lap and the time, returning the card to its initial state.

See it in the guide →

How do I change a card’s name or color?

Tap the name on the card to rename it (up to 6 characters). For the color, tap the total time to open the card detail screen, then use the color icon at the top right to pick one of the 16 presets.

All-Card Controls

How do I start everyone at once?

Use “All Start” in the control bar at the top of the screen. The moment you tap becomes the shared origin, so every affected card starts at exactly the same instant. It applies to cards that are idle or paused, and leaves running cards untouched.

See it in the guide →

Why is an all-card button greyed out?

An all-card button is disabled when no card matches it. All Stop and All Lap apply to running cards, All Start applies to idle and paused cards, and All Reset applies to paused cards. All Reset is the only one that asks for confirmation.

See it in the guide →

Can I control cards team by team?

Yes. Press and hold a card and rest it on another card for about half a second to make a group, or select several cards in Edit mode and tap “Group”. The Start, Stop, Lap and Reset buttons on the group header apply only to the cards in that group. Tap the group name in the header to change its name, color and target laps.

See it in the guide →

How do I break up a group?

Press and hold the card, carry it to a card outside the group and release without resting on it. You can also select cards in Edit mode and tap “Ungroup”. A group disappears on its own once it is empty. No card and no running time is ever lost, and you can move cards in and out while timing.

Laps

What is the “current lap”?

Lap 1 starts as the current lap the moment you press Start. It is the interval still in progress, and stopping does not close it — only pressing Lap does. The current lap is always included in the total, the lap count, the average and anything you export.

See it in the guide →

What do the green and red laps mean?

Green marks the fastest lap and red the slowest. Only laps closed with Lap are considered, and the colors appear once at least two laps have been closed. The current lap is never marked. If several laps share the same time, all of them are colored.

See it in the guide →

Can I set the number of laps in advance?

Yes. Tap the flag button in the all-card bar to set a target, and every card on that screen shows the current lap against it, such as “2/5”. To use a different target for one runner, open the card detail and tap the flag icon there. Reaching the target turns the badge green but never stops timing — extra laps keep showing as “7/5”, and Lap and Stop work exactly as before.

See it in the guide →

How many laps can one card hold?

999 per card, including the current lap. Once you reach the limit only the Lap button is disabled; timing continues as normal.

Screens & Templates

How do Standard, Mini and Rich differ?

Standard shows one easy-to-read card per row, Mini packs two columns for a wider overview, and Rich adds a photo to each card. Each screen keeps its own separate set of up to 50 cards, so a card added on Standard does not appear on Mini.

See it in the guide →

What are templates for?

Save a card setup you use often (names, colors, order and grouping) and recall it next time. Templates do not belong to a screen, so a setup saved on Standard can be recalled on Mini. Recalling one deletes every card and lap on the current screen and replaces them, so save any results you need first. Target laps are not part of a template.

See it in the guide →

Saving & Sharing

How do I keep my results?

Choose “Save Data” from the share icon to store the current names, colors, laps and totals on your device. The label defaults to the date and time, and you can rewrite it right there. Saving does not stop timing. Saved data is listed under Settings > Saved Data.

See it in the guide →

How do I export as CSV or TXT?

From the share icon, pick “Share as CSV”, “Share as TXT” or “Copy as Text”. CSV suits spreadsheets such as Excel, while TXT and the copied text are meant to be read as-is. All of them include the current lap as the final lap at the moment of export.

Tips & Troubleshooting

Is it safe to switch apps or lock the screen?

Yes. Timing is computed from real elapsed time since the start, so it stays accurate in the background and while the screen is locked. Even if the OS terminates the app, the state is restored from the real clock on the next launch. Changing the device date or time manually will skew the results, though.

The screen keeps turning off while I am timing

Turn on “Keep screen on while timing” in Settings. The screen stays awake while any card is running and is released once every card stops. Timing continues either way, even with the screen off.

The time jumped back to 00:00.00

The highest time that can be shown is 99:59.59. Past 100 hours only the displayed and exported value wraps back to 00:00.00 — the measurement itself keeps running.

I cannot type symbols or spaces in a card name

Card names accept only letters, digits and Japanese characters. Symbols, spaces and emoji are stripped as you type. The limit is 6 characters.

I want to start over completely

“Delete All Data” in Settings removes every card, lap, saved record and photo across all three screens. Your settings are kept. This cannot be undone.

Data & Privacy

Where is my timing data stored?

Everything stays on your device. The app never communicates over a network and never sends results, card names or photos to any server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will my data move to a new phone?

Not on Android: the app is excluded from automatic backup and device-to-device transfer. On iOS, if you use device backup such as iCloud Backup, your data comes along when you restore or use Quick Start. Export anything you need to keep as CSV or text before switching.

Does it work without a signal?

Every feature of the app works offline. The How to Use, FAQ, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links in Settings open pages on this website, though, so they need a connection. Open them once beforehand if you want to read them at the track.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and start timing right away. The app does not handle any account information.

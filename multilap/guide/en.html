How to Use

The screen below is a working demo that behaves like the app. Follow the green hints to walk through the basic flow — from timing to reviewing and sharing results — in about a minute.

Learn it in 6 taps

Progress0 / 6
  1. Tap Start on Alex’s cardTiming begins. Lap 1 starts as the “current lap” at the same moment.
  2. Tap Lap twiceEach tap closes the interval on the spot and starts the next lap. The badge shows the lap in progress.
  3. Tap All Start at the topMia and Sam, who haven’t started yet, start at exactly the same instant. Alex keeps running untouched.
  4. Tap Alex’s timeThe card detail opens. Among closed laps, the fastest is green and the slowest is red, with the average shown.
  5. Go back with “<”Timing never stops while you look at the detail. The clock is still running when you return.
  6. Tap the share iconSave the data, copy it as text, or share it as CSV or TXT. The current lap is included.
Nicely done — that’s the basic flow. Feel free to try Stop, Reset, All Lap and the rest.

The card

Reading a card

Each card is one person’s stopwatch. Add cards with the “+” at the bottom of the list.

Swipe a card left to delete it, or press and hold to drag it into a new position.

  1. Name — tap to rename (up to 6 characters).
  2. Total — time since the start. Tap it to open the card detail; timing keeps running.
  3. Current lap — the badge is the lap in progress, followed by that lap’s time. With a target it reads 3/5.
  4. Buttons — Start / Stop on the left, Lap (running) / Reset (paused) on the right.

States

Three states and their buttons

A card is always idle, running or paused, and its buttons change to match.

Idle
Shows 00:00.00. Only Start works
Running
Stop pauses, Lap closes a lap
Paused
Start resumes, Reset clears it
  • Start begins timing and Lap 1
  • Lap closes the lap and starts the next
  • Stop pauses (does not close the lap)
  • Start resumes where it stopped
  • Reset clears all laps and time (no prompt)

The interval still in progress is the “current lap”. It is always included in the total, the lap count, the average and every export.

Groups

Control cards team by team

Grouped cards get a header whose Start, Stop, Lap and Reset apply only to that group. You can group and ungroup even while timing.

  1. Press and hold a card and rest it on another card for about half a second to make a group, or select cards in Edit and tap “Group”.
  2. Header buttons act on the group’s cards only, following the same rules as the all-card bar.
  3. Tap the group name to set its name, color and target laps. ▼ collapses it.
  4. Carry a card outside and release it to ungroup. Empty groups disappear on their own.

Target laps

Set target laps in advance

Register how many laps to run with the flag button in the all-card bar, and badges show “lap / target”.

Alex
05:16.73
5/500:31.06
2/5Running→5/5Green on the last lap→7/5Keeps counting

Reaching the target never stops timing, and Lap and Stop work as usual. Override it per card from the card detail or per group in group settings (card → group → screen, in that order).

Save & share

Save and share results

Keep a record on the spot from the share icon. Saving or exporting never stops timing.

  • Save Data — Stores the results on your device. Find them later under Settings > Saved Data. The label defaults to the date and time.
  • Copy as Text — Puts readable text on the clipboard, ready to paste into a chat or a note.
  • Share as CSV — For spreadsheets such as Excel.
  • Share as TXT — Shares a plain text file.

Sharing from a timing screen covers every card on it; sharing from the card detail covers that card only.

Settings

Settings worth knowing

Keep screen on while timing

Keeps the screen awake only while a card is running (on by default).

Haptics and sounds

Vibration and a tick when you press buttons. Sounds are off by default.

Language and theme

Follow the device, or choose 日本語 / English and light / dark. This website follows the app’s choice too.

Delete All Data

Removes every card, lap, saved record and photo on all three screens. Settings are kept.

Switching apps or locking the screen is fine: timing is calculated from the real clock, so it stays accurate.

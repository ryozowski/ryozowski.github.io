Features

Independent timers

Each person gets a card with its own stopwatch, accurate even after the app is closed.

All-card controls and groups

Start everyone at the same instant, or control each team’s group at once.

Three screens

Easy-to-read Standard, two-column Mini and photo-based Rich. Up to 50 cards each.

Target laps

Set how many laps to run; cards turn green when they reach it.

Save and share

Fastest laps in green, slowest in red. Share as CSV or text on the spot.

Templates

Save your roster and load it again at the next practice.

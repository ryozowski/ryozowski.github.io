Our position

MultiLap (the "App") does not collect any personal information or usage data.

The App does not communicate over a network. All data, including measurement results, card names, and photos, is stored only on the user's device and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

Information we do not collect

The App neither obtains nor transmits any of the following.

  • Personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, IMEI, and the like)
  • Location data
  • Data belonging to other apps on the device, such as contacts, calendar, or call history
  • Usage statistics, crash logs, or analytics of any kind

The App contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

On Android, the App does not declare the INTERNET permission, which guarantees at the platform level that it cannot communicate over a network.

Information stored on the device

The following is stored only on the user's device, solely to make the App work.

Stored dataPurpose
Card names, accent colors, ordering, and group structureTo restore the layout of the measurement screens
Measurement state (start time, accumulated time, laps)To continue and restore timing after the App is closed
Saved records (snapshots of results and their labels)To let users review past results
Templates (reusable card layouts)To reuse a card layout repeatedly
Settings (language, theme, screen wakelock, haptics, sound)To carry settings over to the next launch
Photos assigned to cards on the Rich screenTo make cards easier to tell apart

Uninstalling the App removes all of this from the device.

The App also excludes this data from Android's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer (allowBackup="false" and dataExtractionRules). Data is not carried over when switching devices.

Camera and photo library

The App requests access to the camera or the photo library only when the user assigns a photo to a card on the Rich screen. It never requests access at launch.

Images obtained this way are stored only within the App's area on the device and are never transmitted. Declining the permission leaves every feature other than photos fully usable.

Disclosure to third parties

Because the App collects no data, there is nothing to disclose to third parties.

Deleting data

Users can delete their data in the following ways.

  • Delete individual cards, saved records, or templates from within the App
  • Use "Delete all data" in Settings to remove all measurement data, saved records, and templates
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all data from the device

Children's privacy

The App may be used at any age, and collects no personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the App, please use the contact shown on the support page.

Getting started

  1. Create a fake contact

    On the "Contacts" tab, tap "+" and set the caller name, icon, phone number, ringtone and call screen style. There is no save button. Once you enter a caller name, your changes are saved automatically.

    NameMom
    Phone Number(555) 123-4567
    RingtoneStandard

  2. Get a call right now

    Tap the green call button on the right of a fake contact's card. The incoming call screen opens right away.

    M Mom(555) 123-4567

  3. Schedule a call

    On the "Fake Call Schedules" tab, tap "+" and choose "Timer" (1 to 60 minutes later) or "Set Time". Pick the time with the wheels and tap "Confirm Schedule". You can schedule up to 10 calls, and each one rings at its own time.

    With Set Time, turn on "Repeat" to get the call at the same time every day, or on the same day of the week every week.

    in 15:00 Mom · Standard Phone (iOS) TimerSet Time

  4. Answer the call

    When you answer, the in-call screen opens. It ends automatically after the fake contact's "Auto End Call" time (1 minute by default, 30 seconds to 5 minutes). To end it earlier, tap the red button.

    Mom00:12

When the app is closed, a notification arrives at the scheduled time. Tap it to open the incoming call screen. Because of how iOS works, apps can't show the call screen by themselves while closed. When the app is open, the call screen opens automatically at the scheduled time.

Make it look real

Call screen style

Choose Standard Phone, Messenger Style, Video Call Style or Intl Messenger Style for each fake contact. Standard Phone matches your device.

Background image

Pick a photo, and it fills the incoming call and in-call screens instead of the icon.

Ringtone

Use one of the 4 built-in ringtones, or import your own audio files. The "Default Ringtone" in Settings is used for new fake contacts.

Voice during call

An imported recording can play as the caller's voice 1 second after you answer. If you allow the microphone, it plays through the earpiece (nothing is recorded).

Home screen widget

iOS 17 or later. Tap it to get a call right away from the fake contact you picked.

Touch and hold the app icon

Touch and hold the app icon to see your recently used fake contacts (up to 4). Pick one to get a call right away.

Choose how it rings

Choose how the ringtone and vibration work in Settings > Fake call sound.

The ringtone in the app plays at the same volume as music and videos. If it doesn't ring, see "Sound & vibration" in the FAQ.

Ads and pricing

Every feature is free. There are no in-app purchases.

A banner ad appears at the bottom of the home and settings screens. A full-screen ad appears every second time you return to the home screen after a fake call. No ads appear before a call, while it rings or during the call. Ads are muted.

In Settings > Watch a video to hide ads for 24 hours, watch the video to the end, and no ads are shown for 24 hours.

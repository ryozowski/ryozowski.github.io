How calls work

Will I really get a phone call? Does it cost anything?

No. Both the incoming call and the call itself are recreated on this app's screen. No phone line is used, so there are no call charges.

Will a scheduled call come when the app is closed?

Yes. Even when the app is closed, you get a notification with the ringtone at the scheduled time. Tap the notification to open the incoming call screen.

Because of how iOS works, apps can't show the call screen by themselves while closed. When the app is open, the call screen opens automatically at the scheduled time.

Notifications won't arrive unless you allow them. Allow them in Settings app > Fake Call > Notifications.

How long does the incoming call screen stay?

If you neither answer nor decline, it closes after 30 seconds. After you answer, the in-call screen ends automatically after the fake contact's "Auto End Call" time (1 minute by default, 30 seconds to 5 minutes).

Do the buttons on the in-call screen work?

Mute, Speaker and Keypad respond when tapped. Mute stops the in-call voice, and Speaker plays the in-call voice through the speaker. Numbers you tap on the keypad show on the screen, but no tones play.

Add Contact, Video and More are for looks only. Nothing happens when you tap them.

Schedules

My scheduled call didn't come

Please check:

  • Notifications are allowed (Settings app > Fake Call > Notifications)
  • A Focus such as Do Not Disturb isn't silencing notifications
  • Another fake call screen wasn't showing at the scheduled time. In that case the call doesn't ring, and it isn't rung again later
  • You haven't scheduled several calls for the same time. While the app is open, only the one higher in the list rings

When the app is closed, you get a notification instead of the call screen. Tap the notification to open the incoming call screen.

How many calls can I schedule? How far ahead?

Up to 10, and each one rings at its own time.

  • Timer: 1 to 60 minutes later. It counts from when you save it, and starts over if you save it again
  • Set Time: up to 365 days ahead
Can a call repeat every day or every week?

With Set Time, turn on "Repeat" to get the call at the same time every day or every week. Weekly repeats on the day of the week of the date you picked (you can't choose the day of the week). Timers can't repeat.

What happens to a schedule after it rings?

A one-time schedule is removed from the list automatically after it rings. So is one whose time passed without ringing, for example because the device was off. Repeating schedules stay and ring again next time.

Deleting a fake contact removes all of its schedules, including repeating ones.

If I miss the notification, can I tap it later?

Yes. Tap a notification left in Notification Center to get a call from that fake contact. However, notifications for one-time schedules are removed together with the schedule when you open the app.

Sound & vibration

The app is open, but the ringtone doesn't play

Please check:

  • Silent Mode: With the default "Follow silent mode", the ringtone doesn't play while your device is in Silent Mode, and only vibration is used. To always play it, choose "Always sound and vibration" in Settings > Fake call sound
  • Fake call sound: If "Always vibration only" or "Always no sound or vibration" is selected, the ringtone doesn't play
  • Volume: The ringtone in the app plays at the same volume as music and videos. If the volume is at 0 or low, turn it up with the volume buttons on your device
  • Earphones: If Bluetooth earphones or a speaker are connected, the sound plays there
When the app is closed, the notification makes no sound

When the app is closed, the ringtone plays as an iOS notification sound. The app's "Fake call sound" setting doesn't apply. Your device settings do. Please check:

  • "Sounds" is on in Settings app > Fake Call > Notifications
  • Your device isn't in Silent Mode. Notification sounds don't play in Silent Mode
  • A Focus such as Do Not Disturb isn't silencing notifications
  • The "Ringtone and Alerts" volume in Settings app > Sounds & Haptics isn't too low
I chose a custom ringtone, but the standard one plays when the app is closed

iOS has rules for notification sounds: only certain file formats, and up to 30 seconds long. So when the app is closed, the notification uses the Standard ringtone even if you chose a custom ringtone. If you chose one of the 4 built-in ringtones, that ringtone plays.

When you tap the notification and the call screen opens, your custom ringtone plays.

When the app is closed, the ringtone stops partway

iOS notification sounds can be up to 30 seconds long, so it stops after about 30 seconds. Tap the notification to open the call screen. It rings there until you answer or decline (up to 30 seconds).

My device doesn't vibrate

Make sure "Always no sound or vibration" isn't selected in Settings > Fake call sound.

Vibration also follows your device settings. If haptics are set not to play in Settings app > Sounds & Haptics, your device may not vibrate.

I can't hear the voice during the call
  • The voice plays once, 1 second after you answer. When it ends, the call continues in silence
  • If you allowed the microphone, it plays through the earpiece. Hold the phone to your ear like a real call. To play it through the speaker, tap "Speaker" on the in-call screen
  • It doesn't play while "Mute" is on
  • Check that a voice is selected in "Voice during call" on the fake contact's edit screen

Fake contacts & audio files

How many fake contacts can I add?

Up to 10.

Which audio file formats can I use? How many can I import?

m4a, mp3, wav and aac.

Custom ringtonesUp to 5MB each, up to 20 files
Voice during callUp to 10MB each, up to 50 files

On the fake contact's edit screen, open "Ringtone" or "Voice during call", then import with "Add Custom Ringtone" or "Add Voice File".

Can new fake contacts always get the same ringtone?

The ringtone you choose in Settings > Default Ringtone is used for new fake contacts. You can also choose an imported custom ringtone. Existing fake contacts keep their ringtones.

How do I change the look of the call screen?

On the fake contact's edit screen, choose Standard Phone, Messenger Style, Video Call Style or Intl Messenger Style in "Call Screen Template". Standard Phone matches your device.

Can I use a background image?

Yes. Set one on the fake contact's edit screen, and it fills the incoming call and in-call screens instead of the icon.

Widget & shortcuts

How do I use the home screen widget?

It works on iOS 17 or later.

  1. Touch and hold the home screen, tap "Edit" > "Add Widget" at the top left (on iOS 17, tap "+"), choose "Fake Call" and add it
  2. Touch and hold the widget, tap "Edit Widget" and pick a fake contact

Tap the widget to open the app and get a call from that fake contact right away. If you haven't picked one, it shows the fake contact you used last (or the first one in the list if you haven't used any).

Can I start a call right from the app icon?

Yes. Touch and hold the app icon to see your recently used fake contacts (up to 4). Pick one to get a call from that fake contact right away.

Ads & pricing

Does it cost anything?

No. Every feature is free. There are no in-app purchases.

When do ads appear?

A banner ad appears at the bottom of the home and settings screens. A full-screen ad appears every second time you return to the home screen after a fake call. No ads appear before a call, while it rings or during the call. Ads are muted.

Can I hide the ads?

In Settings > Watch a video to hide ads for 24 hours, watch the video to the end. No ads are shown for 24 hours from then. If you close the video before the end, ads are not hidden.

If you see "The video can't be loaded right now", please try again later.

Data & privacy

Where is my data stored?

Fake contacts, schedules, images and recordings are stored only on this device. Nothing is sent to an outside server. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

What is microphone access used for?

It lets the voice during a call play through the earpiece. Nothing is recorded. If you don't allow it, the voice plays through the speaker.

How do I delete all my data?

In Settings > Delete All Data, you can delete fake contacts, schedules and imported files all at once. Settings such as language and appearance, and your ad-free time, are kept.

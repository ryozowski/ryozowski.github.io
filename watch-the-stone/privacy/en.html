Our Approach

Watch the Stone (the "App") obtains information about users only to the extent described in this policy, and uses it only for the purposes described here.

All of the App's watching and collection features work entirely on your device. The developer of the App does not operate any servers and has no means of receiving your data.

The App communicates over the network only to fetch the advertisement shown at the bottom of the screen. Ads are served by Google's ad service (Google AdMob), and when an ad is fetched, the advertising SDK sends the information described below to Google.

Information Collected and How It Is Used

The developer of the App never obtains your information directly. The following information is collected automatically by the advertising SDK built into the App (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and sent to Google.

InformationHow it is obtainedPurposeSent to
IP address (used to estimate your approximate location)AutomaticallyServing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraudGoogle (Google AdMob)

Diagnostic information (ad load results, performance, and crash information)AutomaticallyInvestigating and improving the advertising SDKGoogle (Google AdMob)
App Set ID (Android only; an identifier shared among apps from the same developer)AutomaticallyMeasuring ad performance, preventing fraudGoogle (Google AdMob)

This information is not used for any purpose other than those listed above. Google retains and handles this information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

The App shows ads under the following policy:

  • Only non-personalized ads are shown. The App does not request ads based on your interests (personalized ads)
  • The Android version removes the Advertising ID permission and the Privacy Sandbox permissions (ad ID, attribution, and topics) from the App, and does not send the Advertising ID
  • The iOS version does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App does not ask for consent, and only Google's "limited ads" (ads that do not use personal data for personalization and do not use device identifiers) are served

Information Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device for the App's features. It is never sent to the developer of the App.

What is storedPurpose
Observation records for the collection (types of changes observed, number of observations, first and last observation dates)To display the collection
Sound on/off settingTo keep your setting the next time you open the App
The watch in progress (the object being watched and the watching time so far)To resume where you left off when the OS temporarily closes the App (kept only temporarily, using the OS's state restoration)

This data is removed from your device when you uninstall the App.

This data may be included in the OS backup features (iCloud Backup / Android Auto Backup). If you restore a backup to a new device, your observation records are carried over. This is a feature of the OS, and nothing is received by the developer of the App.

Device Permissions

The App does not use any permissions that require your approval (camera, photos, location, notifications, microphone, contacts, etc.). It never shows a permission request dialog, including at launch.

The Android version declares permissions such as internet access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, etc., which do not require your approval) in order to fetch ads.

Security Measures

The developer of the App does not operate any servers and does not hold any of your information. Transmission to Google by the advertising SDK is protected by encrypted communication (TLS).

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data as follows:

  • Uninstall the App (all data on your device is removed)

Information held by Google can be managed and deleted through your Google Account settings or by the methods described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Requests for Disclosure, Correction, or Suspension of Use
